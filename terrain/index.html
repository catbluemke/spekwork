Interactive AR Remix of Jane Veeder's WARPITOUT

Jane Veeder pioneered early interactive graphics work, pushing the interactivity of video games into interactive self-portraits. Veeder's WARPITOUT was an early selfie-machine I encountered at the Ontario Science Centre as a child, which enabled dynamic interaction with one's image and explored new grounds for computer graphics and foreshadowed the billion dollar selfie industry of social media. My remix, "Facial Terrain" uses mobile augmented reality to revisit the technological landscape of the self portrait. The face is the latest ground for America's emerging technologies, with face-based hard- and software initiating questions of privacy and ownership of one's image. Facial Terrain takes Veeder's facial territorialization into the mixed reality of 2018.
