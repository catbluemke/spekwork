Phage
Virtual reality application and custom headset
2018

Phage is a virtual reality application and installation in the corollary exhibition to the United States pavilion at the Venice Architecture Biennale 2018. The project explores the spread of the Biennale model from Venice across the globe and speculates: is the Venice Biennale a plague?

The project is in development for public release, it can be followed here.
