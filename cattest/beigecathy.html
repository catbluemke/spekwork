Beige Cathy (performance character)
Performance series
2013-

Beige Cathy is a performance art character who embodies the concept of technology as ‘neutral.’ The character is used to explore new hard/software and the power structures and assumptions that lay its foundations. Beige Cathy has also appeared as a character within the Tough Guy Mountain performance art collective universe since 2013.

Select videos are from Stream Subjectivities exhibited at Mana Contemporary's No Escape Hole (2019) and CBC Arts' This Art Works! (2018).
