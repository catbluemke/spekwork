SpekWork
Video game and interactive media collective
2019-

SpekWork Studio is a collective researching the concepts of work and play in contemporary entertainment media. They develop video games, virtual and augmented reality, and mobile applications that explore the connections between the subject of the user, worker, and player. Studio members Cat Bluemke, Jonathan Carroll, and Ben McCarthy are post-secondary instructors teaching from the intersections of art, labour, and emerging technologies.

Cat Bluemke is co-founder and designer of the studio. The studio’s work can be viewed online at their website and is also available for download on their Itch.io page.
