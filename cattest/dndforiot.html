Dungeons and Dragons for the Internet of Things
Performance, publication and 3D prints
2018-

Play as Google or Alexa on a quest to save the condominium kingdom’s lost princess while exploring the history of domestic labour politics and feminized technologies. Performed within Chicago’s IoT startup incubator, the game targets IoT developers to reflect upon the consequences of pervasive computing. The project was produced by support from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and the Connectory. It was performed over several weeks at the Connectory’s location inside Chicago’s Merchandise Mart in the spring of 2018. The project is currently in development for public release, it’s development can be followed here.
