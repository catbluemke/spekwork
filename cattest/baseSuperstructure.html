Base and Superstructure
SLA 3D printing, publication
2019-

Base and Superstructure uses the language of 3D printing to understand the Marxist concept of human society. The project deconstructs the Marxist concept of the base (forces and relations of production) and the superstructure (culture, politics, institutions).

In 2019, the project was released as a publication through Successful Press for the exhibition unscheduled arrivals at Bunker 2, Toronto.
